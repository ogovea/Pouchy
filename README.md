Pouchy is a tiny, friendly shelf that lives on your Windows desktop and holds things for you while you move around. Shake your mouse, a little pouch appears, you drop a file into it, and then you carry that file wherever you need it — another folder, an email, a chat window, a form on a website. No more dragging a file across three screens while holding your mouse button down and praying you do not let go too early.

It is a native Windows 10 and Windows 11 app with a clean, modern look that fits right in with the rest of your system. Think of it as a temporary pocket for your stuff.

## ✨ What Is Pouchy?

Have you ever tried to move a photo from one folder to another, but the two folders were open in different windows, and dragging between them felt like a juggling act? Or maybe you wanted to attach five files to an email, but they were scattered in four different places on your computer.

That is exactly the problem Pouchy solves.

Pouchy gives you a small floating shelf — a "pouch" — that you can park files, folders, images, and links on. You collect everything you need in one spot first, then drop the whole batch where it belongs. It is a small idea that changes how you work with files day to day.

The app is built for regular people, not programmers. There is nothing to configure, no account to create, and no cloud service to sign up for. It just sits quietly in the background until you need it.

## 🖱️ How to Use Pouchy Day to Day

**Parking an item.** Drag any file, folder, or link and hover it over the open pouch. The pouch will highlight to show it is ready. Let go, and the item is parked.

**Taking an item out.** Drag the item from the pouch to wherever you want it — a folder window, an email draft, a chat message, an upload box on a website. The pouch stays open behind you so you can grab the next item.

**Parking several things at once.** Select multiple files in a folder, then drag them all onto the pouch together. Pouchy holds the whole group.

**Clearing the pouch.** Finished with a task? Clear everything out in one go so the shelf is empty and ready for next time. Nothing is deleted from your computer — clearing the pouch just removes the shortcuts, not your actual files.

**Hiding the pouch.** Click away, press Escape, or drag the last item out. The pouch slips back into the background until you shake your mouse again.

## ⚙️ Making Pouchy Yours

Pouchy is designed to be adjusted without any technical know-how. From the tray icon you can open the settings and change how the pouch behaves — for example, how sensitive the mouse shake needs to be, and how the pouch appears on screen.

If the shake gesture ever feels too eager and triggers when you did not mean it, turn the sensitivity down a notch. If it feels sluggish, turn it up. Small adjustments make a big difference once Pouchy becomes part of your routine.

You can also choose whether Pouchy starts automatically when you turn on your computer. Leaving that on means the pouch is always one shake away, with no extra steps.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

**The pouch does not appear when I shake my mouse.**
Make sure Pouchy is actually running — check for its icon near your clock. If it is there, open settings and raise the shake sensitivity.

**I cannot find Pouchy after installing.**
It lives in the system tray, not on the taskbar. Click the small arrow (^) near your clock to reveal hidden tray icons.

**Dragging an item out does not work.**
Some programs (certain web browsers and a few older apps) do not accept files dropped onto them. Try dropping into a normal folder window first to confirm the item is fine, then drag from that folder.

**The pouch feels laggy.**
Close any very large folder windows you have open and try again. Pouchy itself is lightweight, but huge folders can slow down dragging in Windows generally.

**I want to stop it from starting with Windows.**
Open the Pouchy settings from the tray icon and switch off the automatic start option.

## 🧹 Removing Pouchy

If you ever want to remove it, open Windows Settings, go to **Apps**, find Pouchy in the list, and choose **Uninstall**. Because Pouchy only holds shortcuts to your files while it is running, uninstalling it will not touch any of your documents, photos, or folders. Clearing the pouch before you uninstall is a tidy habit, but nothing is lost either way.
